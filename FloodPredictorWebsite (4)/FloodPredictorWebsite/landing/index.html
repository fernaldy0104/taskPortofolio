How Our AI Works

Our flood prediction system uses cutting-edge machine learning technology to analyze data from multiple sources and predict flood risks with high accuracy.

1

Data Collection

We gather real-time data from government agencies, private companies, and environmental monitoring stations across Jakarta including rainfall, water levels, and infrastructure data.

2

CSV Processing

All collected data is standardized and compiled into structured CSV files with 16 key features including topography, drainage systems, urbanization, and climate factors.

3

Random Forest Model

Our Random Forest algorithm processes the data through multiple decision trees, analyzing complex patterns and relationships between variables to generate accurate predictions.

4

Risk Prediction

The model outputs flood risk levels (Low, Medium, High, Critical) with confidence scores and probability percentages for each district in Jakarta, updated in real-time.
